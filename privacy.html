DeepL-oFM

Privacy policy

This policy covers DeepL-oFM for Mac and iPhone. The app has no account, advertising or analytics. The project maintainers do not collect the text or documents you translate.

Translation providers

The app sends the text you submit to the server you choose. If that server runs on your Mac, processing can stay there. A shared server or cloud provider receives the text and processes it under its own privacy policy. If you enable a fallback on Mac, the app can send the text to that provider when the first one fails.

Data on your device

Apple Keychain stores the API keys you enter. The app stores settings on your device. On Mac, you can disable history in Settings and erase existing entries in History. The Files tab creates a temporary translated copy on your Mac before you save it. On iPhone, the app does not keep a translation history.

Permissions

On Mac, the selection shortcut uses Accessibility to copy selected text after you press the shortcut. On iPhone, Local Network permission allows the app to connect to a translation server on your Wi-Fi network. Sharing and pasting run when you choose those actions.

App updates

The Mac app checks for updates on GitHub. On iPhone, Apple handles downloads and updates through the App Store.

Contact

Use the project's GitHub page to ask a privacy question. For a sensitive issue, use GitHub’s private security report.
